This repository documents the research, parts, procedures, and verified community references for converting a 1997 Volkswagen Eurovan VR6 (Winnebago Camper) from the factory 01P automatic transmission to the European‑market 02G 5‑speed manual transmission.

The 01P automatic is widely regarded as the Achilles heel of the VR6 Eurovan platform. This project consolidates the proven path to eliminating that failure mode permanently.

I have a '97 van. I purchased it at ~20k mi. Had an initial automatic transmission failure ~20k mi later at 40K mi, despite doing a preventative transmission fluid service upon acquisition. I then replaced the transmission, and replaced the tiny cigarette sized trans cooler package with a legit radiator sitting in front of the stock engine radiator. And monitored the transmission temp with TorquePro via Android. Nonetheless, ~40mi later at just over 80k miles, i again experienced a transmission failure. I don't have the stomach to swap it with another automatic trans-- i am considering a manual swap and thus am compiling this research.

## 🧩 Why This Matters

The VR6 Eurovan is a uniquely capable platform — compact, powerful, and ideal for Winnebago’s camper conversion. Its primary systemic weakness, in my ownership experience over more than a decade, is the 01P automatic transmission.

The 02G manual swap:

- Eliminates the platform’s #1 failure point  
- Improves reliability, drivability, and serviceability  
- Extends the life of the van dramatically  
- Now (as of 2026) appears to be **proven**, **repeatable**, and **well‑documented**  

This repository exists to make that path accessible.
