# VR6 Eurovan (12‑Valve) Manual Transmission Swap
## Torque Specs, Fasteners, and Critical Checks

This document consolidates the essential torque values, fastener notes, and “must‑check” items for a VR6 → 02G manual swap.

---

## 1. Engine → Transmission Interface

### Bellhousing Bolts (VR6 → 02G)
- **M12 bolts:** 60 Nm (44 ft‑lb) + 90°
- **M10 bolts:** 45 Nm (33 ft‑lb)

### Starter Motor Bolts
- **M10:** 45 Nm (33 ft‑lb)

---

## 2. Flywheel & Clutch

### Flywheel Bolts (VR6)
- **New bolts required**
- 60 Nm (44 ft‑lb) + 90°  
- Tighten in star pattern

### Pressure Plate Bolts
- 20 Nm (15 ft‑lb)  
- Tighten evenly in sequence

---

## 3. Axles & Driveline

### Axle Nut (Front)
- **VR6 manual:** 300 Nm (221 ft‑lb)  
- Re‑torque after first drive

### Inner CV Bolts (to transmission flange)
- **M8 triple‑square:** 40 Nm (30 ft‑lb)
- Use blue Loctite

---

## 4. Transmission Mounts

### Left/Right Mount Brackets
- **M10 bolts:** 45 Nm (33 ft‑lb)

### Transmission → Mount Bolts
- **M12 bolts:** 60 Nm (44 ft‑lb)

### Subframe Mount Bolts (if loosened)
- **Front subframe bolts:** 70 Nm (52 ft‑lb) + 90°
- **Rear subframe bolts:** 70 Nm (52 ft‑lb) + 90°

---

## 5. Shifter Assembly

### Shifter Box to Floor
- **M8 bolts:** 25 Nm (18 ft‑lb)

### Shift Cable Brackets
- **M8 bolts:** 25 Nm (18 ft‑lb)

---

## 6. Clutch Hydraulics

### Slave Cylinder Bolts
- **M8:** 25 Nm (18 ft‑lb)

### Master Cylinder Nuts
- **M6:** 10 Nm (7 ft‑lb)

---

## 7. Fluids

### 02G Gear Oil Capacity
- **2.25–2.5 liters** (GL‑4 spec)

### Clutch Hydraulic Fluid
- DOT 4  
- Bleed until firm engagement and no micro‑bubbles

---

## 8. Critical Checks (Do Not Skip)

### Before First Start
- Verify **flywheel bolts** torqued + angle
- Verify **pressure plate** torqued evenly
- Confirm **slave cylinder** is fully seated
- Confirm **axle seals** not pinched
- Confirm **mounts** are fully torqued

### Before First Drive
- Shifter moves through all gates smoothly
- Clutch pedal has full stroke and return
- No leaks at master/slave or hydraulic line
- Reverse lights function

### After First Drive (5–10 miles)
- Re‑torque axle nuts
- Re‑torque inner CV bolts
- Check for seepage at axle seals
- Verify shifter alignment under load

---

## 9. Notes

- All torque values are based on VW T4/VR6/02G factory data ranges.
- Replace any stretch bolts (flywheel, subframe).
- Use anti‑seize on exhaust fasteners and penetrating oil on subframe bolts.
