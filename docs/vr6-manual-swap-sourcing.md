# VR6 Eurovan (12‑Valve) Manual Transmission Swap
## Sourcing Leads & Pricing Targets (U.S. + EU)

This document lists reliable (per initial online research, not personal experience) sources for 02G transmissions, pedal clusters, shifters, mounts, and supporting hardware, along with realistic 2026 pricing targets.

---

- Complete VR6 manual‑swap kits
- 02G transmissions
- Pedal clusters
- Shifter assemblies
- Axles and mounts

# 6. Sourcing Strategy (Recommended)

1. **Buy the transmission first** — availability fluctuates.
2. **Buy pedal cluster + shifter next** — these are the hardest to find in the U.S.
3. **Buy new clutch/flywheel/hydraulics** — inexpensive and worth replacing.
4. **Buy mounts + axles** — either from donor or U.S. suppliers.
5. **Buy emulator last** — always in stock.

---

# 7. Notes

- EU sourcing is cheaper but requires patience and communication.
- U.S. sourcing is faster but more expensive.
- A complete kit from a single seller reduces compatibility headaches.
